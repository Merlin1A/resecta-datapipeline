# Code of Conduct

This code applies in every Resecta project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and any other channel where the project is being worked on — and to anyone taking part, including the maintainers.

## The standard

Be respectful. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing someone's private information are not acceptable here, in public or in private. Disagree about the work, not the person: critique code, designs, and decisions on their merits, and assume good faith in return.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that falls short of this standard, email `security@resecta.app` (the same address used for security reports). Reports are handled in confidence; the reporter's identity is not shared without their consent.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide how to respond. Depending on the case, they may issue a warning, edit or remove content, or exclude someone from project spaces temporarily or permanently. Their decisions are final.
